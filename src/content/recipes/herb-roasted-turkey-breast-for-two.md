Thanksgiving for two doesn't need a whole turkey and a week of leftovers. A single turkey breast roasts in about an hour, stays juicy under a layer of herb butter, and gives you plenty of drippings for real gravy.

**The secret:** a thermometer. Pull the turkey at 160°F and let it rest. That's the difference between juicy and dry. Serve it with our [Sausage, Apple & Herb Stuffing](/recipes/sausage-apple-herb-stuffing-for-two/) and save room for [Pumpkin Crème Brûlée](/recipes/pumpkin-creme-brulee-for-two/).
