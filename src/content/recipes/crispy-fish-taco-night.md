Taco night is the most relaxed kind of date night: no plating stress, lots of choices, and plenty of laughing at overstuffed tortillas.

The homemade **pico de gallo** and smoky **chipotle crema** are what make these tacos special. Both take five minutes, and you can make them up to a day ahead. Leftover crema is great on eggs, burgers or roasted potatoes.
