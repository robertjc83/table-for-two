## Description

Skip the reservation. Cook something together.

**Date Night In** is a 63-page e-cookbook with 12 complete date nights at home. Each one is a full evening already planned for you: what to cook, what to pour, what to play and what to talk about.

Every recipe serves two and splits the work between **Chef 1** and **Chef 2**, so you're cooking side by side instead of one of you doing everything while the other waits.

**Every date night includes:**
🍽️ A complete menu (starter, main, dessert and drink, depending on the night)
⏱️ A step-by-step game plan: what to make ahead and when to start each dish, so everything is ready at the same time
🛒 A shopping list for the whole menu that you can check off at the store
🍷 A drink pairing, a playlist and an idea for setting the mood
💬 Three conversation starters to get you talking about more than your week

**The 12 date nights:**
1. Steakhouse Night
2. A Night in Little Italy
3. Seaside Trattoria
4. Taco Night Fiesta
5. Takeout Fake-Out
6. Weeknight Glow-Up (ready in about 25 minutes)
7. Supper Club Classic
8. Rainy Night In
9. Fall Harvest Supper
10. Slow Sunday in Paris
11. Thanksgiving for Two
12. Christmas Eve for Two

**Also inside:**
• 26 recipes with prep and cook times, difficulty, and Chef 1/Chef 2 steps
• The Date Night Pantry: the staples and gear that make every menu easier
• A printable "Our Date Night Plan" page to fill in together
• A clickable table of contents for easy reading on a tablet or phone

**Good for:**

• A gift for newlyweds, a new couple, or anyone who loves to cook
• Beginners: most recipes are Easy or Medium, and every step says who does what
